Before You Ask

"Isn't AI already doing this?"

AI didn't replace writers — it replaced undifferentiated writing. Generic, keyword-stuffed content that could be about any product in any category is exactly what AI produces on its own, and companies have stopped paying humans for that.

What's gotten more valuable is what AI can't do alone: original research, real subject-matter depth, editorial judgment, and a voice worth trusting.

This program is built around that gap — including teaching you to use AI-assisted drafting deliberately and edit it into something accurate and human, because that's the actual job in most professional writing roles right now.
